Devices are covered by the manufacturer’s or seller’s warranty. The warranty period is stated in each product’s specifications.

What you need for a warranty claim

  • your order number or proof of purchase;
  • a description of the fault and, if possible, photos or video;
  • the device serial number (where applicable).

Process

  1. Write to {warranty_contact} with your order number.
  2. We register the claim and tell you where to hand over the device — a manufacturer service centre or us.
  3. After diagnostics you receive a decision: repair, replacement or refund according to the law.

What is not covered

Mechanical damage, liquid ingress (if the device has no relevant protection), unauthorised repair, natural wear of batteries and consumables.

Your statutory rights

The warranty does not limit your rights under consumer protection law.
