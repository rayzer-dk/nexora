Delivery is calculated at checkout based on your city, parcel weight and the chosen carrier.

Delivery methods

  • Carrier branch or parcel locker. Choose a city and pickup point — no address needed.
  • Courier delivery to your address. Provide street, building and a convenient contact.
  • Store pickup. If the store has a pickup point, it appears among the options.

Timing and cost

In-stock items ship within 1–2 business days after the order is confirmed. For made-to-order items the lead time is shown on the product page. The delivery price is visible before you confirm the order; free delivery applies from the amount shown in the store header.

Receiving your parcel

Check the contents and condition when you receive the parcel. If there is damage, draw up a report with the carrier representative and contact us: {return_contact}.

Tracking

The tracking number is sent in a notification and shown on the order status page.
