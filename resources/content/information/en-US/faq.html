Orders

How do I place an order?

Add an item to the cart, enter your contact details, choose delivery and payment and confirm the order.

Do I need to register?

No. Guest checkout is always available; an account is only needed for order history and saved addresses.

Can I change or cancel an order?

Yes, until it is handed to delivery. Please write to us as early as possible: {email}.

Delivery and payment

How much is delivery?

The price is calculated at checkout; above a certain amount delivery is free.

Is card payment safe?

Yes. Payment happens on the payment provider’s page and card data never reaches the store.

Products

What do “made to order” and “coming soon” mean?

These items are not in stock right now. The product page shows an estimated availability; where offered you can subscribe to a notification.

Is there a warranty?

Yes, the period is stated in the product specifications. See the Warranty page for details.

Data and security

What data do you collect?
