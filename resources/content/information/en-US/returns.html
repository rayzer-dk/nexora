You may return or exchange goods of proper quality in accordance with consumer protection law.

Applicable consumer law: {consumer_law}.

Time limits

Unused goods of proper quality can be returned within 14 days of receipt. For distance purchases you may withdraw from the contract within 14 days. Certain categories (for example goods covered by statutory exceptions or digital goods once the download has started) are not returnable.

Conditions

  • the product keeps its appearance, consumer properties, seals and tags;
  • the complete set and packaging are included;
  • you can provide proof of purchase (order number).

How to return

  1. Submit a request in your account or write to: {return_contact}.
  2. We confirm the return and send shipping instructions.
  3. After we receive and inspect the goods we refund the money via the original payment method.

Refunds

We refund within 14 days after receiving the goods and your return request. The return shipping cost is determined by law and by the order terms.

Defective goods

If you found a defect, see the Warranty page or contact: {warranty_contact}.
