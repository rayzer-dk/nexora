Available payment methods depend on the store settings and the chosen delivery. The final list is shown at checkout.

Payment methods

  • Card online. Payment is processed on a secure page of the payment provider; the store does not receive or store card data.
  • Bank transfer. An invoice is generated automatically and the order is reserved for the period stated in the message.
  • Cash on delivery. Available for selected carriers; the carrier may charge a transfer fee.
  • Company invoice. For B2B customers under an agreement and credit limit.

Currency and exchange rate

Prices are shown in the storefront currency. If you choose another currency, the amount is converted at the store rate and the final amount is fixed in the order.

Confirmation

After payment you receive an order notification. Documents (invoice, waybill) are available in your account.
