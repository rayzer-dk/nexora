Cookies are small files a website stores in your browser. We use them to make the store work, to remember your preferences and — only with your consent — to measure audience and advertising performance.

Applicable rules: {cookie_law}.

Categories

  • Necessary. Cart, session, security and remembering your cookie choice. Always active; no consent required.
  • Preferences. Language, currency, market.
  • Analytics. Helps us understand how the site is used. Off until you agree.
  • Marketing. Personalisation and ad measurement. Off until you agree.

Managing consent

On your first visit we show a banner: you can accept all, reject optional cookies or choose categories. You can change your choice at any time via the “Cookie settings” link at the bottom of the page. Declining does not affect the cart or checkout. You can also delete or block cookies in your browser settings.

Google Consent Mode

If Google integrations are enabled, the analytics and advertising states are passed in Consent Mode: before consent they are “denied”.
