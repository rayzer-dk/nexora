# EU compliance baseline

Core contains only broadly applicable e-commerce requirements: seller identity, privacy/consent, consumer-rights policies, price-reduction history, accessibility baseline and product safety information.

For physical products sold in the EU, the generic product compliance model can store the manufacturer/economic operator, EU responsible person when applicable, model/regulatory identifiers, localized warnings/safety information and supporting product documents.

CE marking and an EU Declaration of Conformity remain available when they apply to the product. Core does not include niche serial/lot/recall systems, energy-label/EPREL workflows, Digital Product Passport storage or sector-specific EPR/WEEE/battery workflows. Those belong in optional extensions so ordinary stores remain small and understandable.
