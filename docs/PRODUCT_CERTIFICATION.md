# Product documents and certification

Core keeps product compliance universal and compact. A product may have manufacturer information, an EU responsible person where applicable, localized warnings/safety information and attached documents.

The baseline document model supports manuals, datasheets, certificates, declarations of conformity and generic test/safety documents without hard-coding niche industry workflows into the product table.

Specialized functionality such as serial/lot tracking, product recalls, energy-label registries, Digital Product Passports or sector-specific registries is not part of Core. It should be provided by optional extensions when a merchant actually needs it.
