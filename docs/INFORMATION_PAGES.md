# Information pages

Core ships with a small universal set of customer-facing information pages. Their visible titles are localized, but the public route keys are stable English paths so integrations, links and upgrades do not depend on transliteration.

Default pages:
- About us: `/about-us`
- Contact: `/contact`
- Shipping: `/shipping`
- Payment: `/payment`
- Returns: `/returns`
- Warranty: `/warranty`
- FAQ: `/faq`
- Privacy Policy: `/privacy-policy`
- Cookie Policy: `/cookie-policy`
- Terms & Conditions: `/terms-and-conditions`

The footer groups them into Company, Customer help and Legal information. Content is editable through the Content Engine. Legal pages are versioned and should not be published until the store identity/contact fields required by the page are completed.

The platform deliberately does not create dozens of niche pages. Product-specific regulatory content belongs to the product/document model or an optional extension rather than the global footer.
