# Legal Document Center

Privacy notice, cookie policy, terms of sale, return/withdrawal policy, warranty policy, accessibility statement and other legal content are versioned entities.

Each document is scoped to a store, translated independently and has an effective period. Checkout and consent records reference the version effective at the time of the action. A later edit therefore creates a new version instead of silently mutating historical evidence.

Documents that materially change consent/contract terms can be marked as requiring re-acceptance. Routine wording corrections can be published without forcing unrelated account actions.
