# Changelog

Nexora Commerce has a single product line on the `main` branch. Only release-level changes are listed.

## 3.70.2 — 2026-10-05

Fixes found by the full end-to-end run.

- The CSV export of shoppers waiting for a product has a `.csv` address like every other export.
- The call-me widget fields carry their own accessible names.
- The builder's schematic preview picks a readable text colour for blocks with a dark background.
- The quality page no longer scrolls sideways on a phone.
