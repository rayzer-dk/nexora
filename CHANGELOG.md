# Changelog

Nexora Commerce has a single product line on the `main` branch. Only release-level changes are listed.

## 3.73.0 — 2026-10-05

A presentation page after installation, a calmer storefront header and a large Russian translation.

- The page shown after a successful installation is now a presentation: a storefront and admin preview, three steps to the first orders, eight feature cards and why the product is convenient (uk, en, ru).
- Storefront: default container 1500 px and five product cards per row on a desktop; the header search is shorter and the shop's phone and e-mail sit to its right; subcategories on a category page are compact round chips instead of product-like cards; the delivery and payment cards of the product tab have a slightly darker background; gift card and bonus fields span the row like the coupon field; the catalog filter hint says "product code".
- Russian: about 480 interface and message texts that fell back to English (the "Add to cart" button, payment names, errors of the cart, checkout, account, returns, reviews, delivery and the information page titles).
- English is shown with the British flag everywhere, flags are rectangular, and the theme switch is one sun-and-moon icon in the admin and the storefront.
- Admin: thumbnails in the lists of categories and articles; the quick-jump search no longer pushes the close button to a second row.
