# Changelog

Nexora Commerce has a single product line on the `main` branch. Only release-level changes are listed.

## 3.49.0 — 2026-10-05

Abandoned carts, editable footer, icon picker, admin audit.

- Abandoned carts keep the contacts: name, phone and e-mail typed into the checkout are saved even if the shopper leaves; the list shows photos of the products, contacts, total, time and a "Remind" button that sends the e-mail at once. The reminder e-mail lists the products with photos; automatic reminders can also go to people who left an e-mail at checkout (switch in the template).
- Footer is editable: newsletter block (icon, texts per language, colours), tagline, copyright line, colours, every built-in link can be hidden, up to three own columns with links.
- Top bar texts, brand, search hint, hero, promo blocks, cookie notice and announcement are written per store language (a form save no longer overwrites other languages).
- Announcement bar: more messages that take turns, text colour, devices; the top service bar can be hidden.
- Icon picker: every Lucide icon in a modal with search; used for the benefit cards and the newsletter block. All modal windows blur the page behind them.
- Admin audit: dates without seconds, translated statuses, payment methods, carriers and reasons; orders, customers (phone, orders, spent), shipments, inquiries, returns, roles and promotions fixed or extended; confirmation dialog buttons.
- Russian storefront texts completed.
