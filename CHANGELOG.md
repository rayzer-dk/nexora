# Changelog

Nexora Commerce has a single product line on the `main` branch. Only release-level changes are listed.

## 3.70.0 — 2026-10-05

E-mail confirmation became a setting, and the layout builder shows the real page.

- A separate switch "confirm the e-mail" for the newsletter and for "notify me when back in stock" (on by default). When it is off, the subscriber or the waiting shopper is active at once. The switch sits next to the list of subscribers and the list of waiting shoppers, where unconfirmed entries are filtered, counted and marked.
- Layout builder: a real preview of the saved draft on the actual storefront page (desktop, tablet, phone widths, signed 30-minute link, not indexed, nothing is published).
- Layout builder: revision statuses are translated, the breadcrumb separator and the wrapping of block names are fixed.
